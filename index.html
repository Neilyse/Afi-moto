Afi moto Privacy Policy

At Afi Moto, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. We only collect essential information, such as your phone number, name, profile details, and real-time location, solely to facilitate ride matching, enable direct communication between drivers and passengers, and ensure platform safety. Your location data is used exclusively to display nearby rides and track active trips, and we never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. All sensitive data is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized systems and administrators for account verification and service delivery.

Who we are

Afi Moto is a moto-taxi app in Rwanda that connects riders with nearby certified Motari drivers. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Afi Moto, "you" means anyone who uses the app as a rider or as a Motari driver.

By creating an account or using Afi Moto, you agree to the practices described here.

What we collect

At Afi Moto, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. We only collect essential information.

  • Phone number and name, to create and identify your account.
  • Profile details you add, such as your profile photo or rider and driver information shown during a ride.
  • Real-time location, from your device while you use the app.
  • Trip information, such as pickup and drop-off points, fare offers, trip status and ratings.
  • Messages and calls made through the app, so riders and Motaris can coordinate a pickup.
  • Verification details for Motari drivers, provided during sign-up so we can verify accounts and run background checks.

How we use it

  • To match riders with the nearest Motaris and deliver ride requests.
  • To enable direct communication between drivers and passengers, by call or in-app chat.
  • To keep the platform safe, including verifying drivers, handling reports and preventing fraud or misuse.
  • To run and improve the service, such as fixing errors and keeping fares and dispatch fair.

We do not use your data for anything unrelated to these purposes, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Your location

Your location is used exclusively to display nearby rides and to track active trips.

  • Riders: your pickup location is sent to Motaris near you when you request a ride, and to your matched Motari once they accept.
  • Motaris: your location is used to show you nearby ride requests, and your matched rider can see it live while you travel to the pickup and during the trip.

You can turn off location access in your device settings at any time. Without it, the app cannot match you with nearby rides.

Who we share it with

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only where it is needed to run a ride:

  • The rider or Motari you are matched with can see your name, profile details, phone number and live trip location.
  • Google Maps. We use Google Maps to show driver arrival, distances and pickup guidance. Google processes the location data needed for these features under its own privacy policy.
  • Authorized administrators and systems that handle account verification and service delivery.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it or when needed to protect someone's safety.

Storage and security

All sensitive data is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized systems and administrators for account verification and service delivery. We limit access to the people who need it and take reasonable technical and organizational steps to guard against loss, misuse and unauthorized access.

No system is perfectly secure. Please keep your phone and account secure, and tell us right away if you think someone else has access to your account.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within [30 days], except for what we must keep to meet legal obligations or resolve safety disputes. Live location is used for the trip in progress and is not used for any other purpose.

Your rights

Under Rwanda's law on the protection of personal data and privacy, you can:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your data and close your account.
  • Withdraw your consent, including location access, at any time.
  • Object to how your data is processed.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can also raise your concern with Rwanda's data protection supervisory authority.

Children

Afi Moto is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the app.
