Afi moto Privacy Policy

At Afi Moto, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. We only collect essential information, such as your phone number, name, profile details, and real-time location, solely to facilitate ride matching, enable direct communication between drivers and passengers, and ensure platform safety. Your location data is used exclusively to display nearby rides and track active trips, and we never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. All sensitive data is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized systems and administrators for account verification and service delivery.
